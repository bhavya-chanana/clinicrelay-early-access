Register, tokens and queue

Appointments and walk-ins in one queue. Find a patient by phone, including family members who share a number. Call the next patient in one tap.

Prescription pad

Your letterhead, your templates and favourites, printed on A4 or A5. Only the doctor signs. Corrections are added as new versions, so the original is never overwritten.

Questions

Is the product ready today?

Not for everyone. The clinic desk is in development, and founding clinics go live in batches as each part is ready. We'll give you a date before we take any monthly payment.

How do refunds work?

Message us any time before you go live and we'll refund the full deposit to the account you paid from. You don't need to give a reason.

What happens to the deposit?

It's taken off your first monthly bill. You're not charged anything else until you go live.

We already use another software. Can we switch?

Yes. We'll import your patient list and set up your fees and templates. The import is free for founding clinics.

Does the AI answer medical questions?

No. It books, reschedules and answers questions about timings and fees. Anything medical goes to your staff, and it never writes or changes a prescription.

Which specialties?

General practice, paediatrics, dermatology and dental clinics with 1–3 doctors fit best. If you're somewhere else, reserve anyway and tell us in the form.
